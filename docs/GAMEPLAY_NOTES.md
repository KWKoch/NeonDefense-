# Gameplay notes: Neon Defense, play 01

What a player experiences, and the numbers behind it. Written for deciding what to tune after the closed test.

## A new player's first 14 days

| Day | What they do | What the game gives |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Play levels 1 to 5 (about 4 minutes each) | Starter cards on every win, a 15-wave first level, daily reward unlocks after the first win |
| 2 onward | Open the game, clear a level, claim the Daily reward | 11 gems, 3 crystals and 10 season points every day, but only after playing |
| After clearing level 5 | Tester welcome package (Shop) | 100 gems, a free Surge Pack, full energy, and Season premium when the season is a free one |
| Every day | Season pass (Shop) | 30 tiers over 30 days. Season points from new clears, replays, new stars and the Daily reward. Premium costs 600 gems |

## Rules in this build
- **Rewards need play.** The Daily reward stays locked until the player clears a level (or survives 5 or more waves) that day.
- **Everyone is a tester for now.** While TESTERS_OPEN is on, every account gets the welcome package once.
- **No ads, no purchases.** Everything is earned. Reroll and Revive-style buttons work immediately; extra rerolls are capped at 2 a run.
- **Gems have three tiers.** Common 1, Rare 4, Exquisite 32 (8 Rare). The wallet is one number, shown as the fewest gems that make it up. Prices are in Common value.
- **Levels 1 to 5 have 15 waves** (was 20). Level 6 onward is unchanged: 20 waves, 30 on boss levels, 40 on level 50.
- **Worlds 2 to 5 are locked** ("Coming in an update"). World 1 is the whole game for now.
- **Speed is remembered** across launches. After clearing level 2, a one-time tip points at the speed button.
- **Icons, not abbreviations.** The commander ability button shows a bolt when ready, instead of the letters "RDY".

## Pacing numbers (calculated, not measured on real players)
- Level 1 at 20 waves was 5.5 minutes with 19 card picks. At 15 waves it should be about 4 minutes with 14 picks.
- Levels 1 to 5 together are about 7 minutes shorter. World 1 is roughly 4.5 hours of play.
- A free player earns about 450 to 650 gems by level 50 plus 100 from the welcome package.

## Things to watch in the closed test
1. **Do levels 1 to 5 now feel too steep?** Each wave in a 15-wave level ramps faster than in a 20-wave one. Ask testers if level 4 or 5 is a wall.
2. **Where do testers stop playing?** Ask for the level number.
3. **Is the Daily lock annoying?** If testers complain they can't claim at once, loosen it to "open the game today".
4. **Does the Season pass pull people back?** Ask testers which tier they reached.
5. **Is the speed tip noticed?** Count how many testers use 2x or 3x.
